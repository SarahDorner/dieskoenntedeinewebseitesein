How junk car removal works

1. You send us the details

Tell us the make, model, year and condition of your car and send a few photos. We reply with a fixed price that is not renegotiated at pickup.

2. We pick up the car

We are often with you within 6 hours. If you are still waiting for your new car, we come on the date that suits you, even several weeks from now. It does not matter whether the car still runs: we get it out of a parking garage, a backyard or even a ditch.

3. You get paid and receive your papers

We pay the residual value on handover, in cash or by instant bank transfer. Your car is recycled by a certified facility under the German End-of-Life Vehicle Ordinance. You receive the official certificate of destruction (Verwertungsnachweis) by WhatsApp, email or mail.

What we need on pickup day

  • Registration certificate part I and part II (Fahrzeugschein and Fahrzeugbrief)
  • All keys you still have
  • Your ID
  • Access to the vehicle

US forces, expats, students

Is your vehicle registered through the US forces or abroad? Tell us when you contact us. We will let you know which papers are needed in your case.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a junkyard near me in Germany?

You do not need one nearby. We pick up your car anywhere in Germany, free of charge. If you prefer to drop it off yourself, call our hotline and we will tell you the nearest drop-off point.

Do I get money for my junk car?

Yes. You always get the residual value of your vehicle. We quote a fixed price in advance and pay on handover.

How fast can you pick up my car?

Often within 6 hours. If you are still waiting for your new car, we come on the date that suits you.

What is a Verwertungsnachweis?

It is the official German certificate of destruction. It confirms that your vehicle was recycled by a certified facility. You get it from us free of charge.

Do you deregister the car for me?

For vehicles registered in Germany we handle the deregistration free of charge and send you the confirmation. If your vehicle is registered differently, for example through the US forces, tell us and we will explain the next step.

My car is parked on the street without registration. What now?

Deregistered vehicles may not stay on public roads. Contact us soon so we can arrange a quick pickup.
